How we charge

No percentage fees. No commissions.

Many designers charge a share of the project cost, so their fee grows with your bill. We don’t. Materials are chosen to fit the budget you set, and you keep complete control over it.

How it works

Three steps

  1. Show us your space

    Visit the showroom at Gorewada Square or share your floor plan. We note your requirements, answer your questions and help you settle on a budget.

  2. Get custom design plans

    Your rooms in 2D and 3D, with a walkthrough video of the finished design. We refine it with you until it’s right.

  3. Select & execute

    Choose materials that fit your budget. Our team carries out the civil work, carpentry and finishing, through to handover.
