## Photographs — permission required before any production use

No photograph is stored in this repository. Each is displayed directly from NEST Architects' own website
(`nestarchitects.in/wp-content/uploads/photo-gallery/…`), so the studio keeps control and can withdraw them.
Copyright sits with NEST Architects and/or their photographers (no photographer is credited on their site or in the
published feature). Before a production site: written licence from the studio, photographer credit and consent,
homeowner consent where relevant, and high-resolution originals (the public files are only ~810 px wide).
